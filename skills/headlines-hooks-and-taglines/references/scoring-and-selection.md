# Scoring and selection

These are practitioner heuristics for forcing consistent comparison, not validated thresholds.

## Selection procedure

1. Write 10+ candidates.
2. Cut with the commandments checklist (aim for 4-6 hits each).
3. Keep 3-5; run the 4 U's (pass at 3/4).
4. Rate survivors on the 5-part rubric.
5. Re-read as the reader: What is in it for me? Too abstract? Would I stop scrolling? Does it make sense standalone?
6. Ship the top 3 with rationale; keep alternates from a different angle.
7. When traffic allows, A/B test the top two in the real channel; real results overrule the rubric.

## Commandments checklist (aim for 4-6 per line)

1. Specific numbers
2. Negativity or loss framing (test; see conflict note in SKILL.md)
3. Pattern interrupt
4. Target callout
5. Problem callout
6. Confidence (no maybe, might, could)
7. Clean visual layout
8. Clear benefit, not process
9. Social proof
10. Warning or urgency (only when real)

Hitting all ten usually produces a cluttered line; fewer, stronger hits win.

## The 4 U's

| U | Question |
|---|---|
| Useful | Does it offer something the reader wants? |
| Urgent | Is there a reason to read now? |
| Unique | Is it distinct from what the reader has seen? |
| Ultra-specific | Does it name concrete detail (number, audience, outcome)? |

Pass: 3 of 4. Example that hits all four (figures illustrative): "7 Little-Known SEO Tricks to Boost Your Traffic by [X]% in Just [N] Days".

## Anatomy rubric (headlines and hooks)

Rate 1-5 each: Clarity, Specificity, Urgency, Uniqueness, Relevance. Max 25. Working pass 20; 22+ is strong. Cutoff is a rule of thumb.

| Dimension | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | Needs context to decode | Understood in one pass |
| Specificity | Generic claim | Number, audience, outcome named |
| Urgency | No reason now | Real, honest reason to act or read now |
| Uniqueness | Seen everywhere | Fresh angle or ownable detail |
| Relevance | Wrong audience | Names the reader's own problem or goal |

## Slogan rubric

Rate 1-5: memorable, clear, differentiating, on-brand, versatile (works across media), timeless. Max 30. Recommend top 3.
