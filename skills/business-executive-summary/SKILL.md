# Executive Summary Generation

Create a comprehensive executive summary of the business validation analysis:

## Summary Structure

1. **Business Overview**
   - Core business model and value proposition
   - Key market opportunity and timing
   - Competitive positioning and differentiation

2. **Validation Findings**
   - Strongest business fundamentals
   - Most concerning assumptions or gaps
   - Key risks and mitigation priorities

3. **Financial Assessment**
   - Revenue projection credibility assessment
   - Unit economics sustainability evaluation
   - Funding requirements and timeline validation

4. **Market Position**
   - Market opportunity verification results
   - Competitive landscape reality check
   - Go-to-market strategy feasibility

5. **Investment Readiness**
   - Material accuracy and completeness
   - Key strengths for investor presentation
   - Critical gaps requiring attention

6. **Recommendations**
   - Top 3 priority improvements
   - Alternative scenario considerations
   - Next steps for validation completion

Provide a balanced, objective assessment suitable for board or advisor review.
