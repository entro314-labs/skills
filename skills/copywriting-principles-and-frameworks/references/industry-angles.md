## Industry angles

| Industry | Angle |
|---|---|
| B2B SaaS | Lead with time saved or revenue gained, quantified; "your team"; speak to the buyer's boss; integrations and security are objections, not headline features (place security badges near CTAs); free trial tends to beat demo request at lower price points |
| Professional services | Credentials and results from similar clients first; case studies are the main asset; answer "do I need this?" before "why you?"; show the process; "when we start", not "if"; price on value, not hours |
| E-commerce/DTC | Lead with the transformation; benefits before specs; sensory language; real customer photos and reviews; real stock and shipping deadlines; size guides reduce returns; mobile-first above the fold |
| Healthcare, legal | Compliance wording required but still plain; credentials over bold claims; education first; no unsubstantiated claims; clear disclaimers |
| Financial services | Regulatory disclaimers are non-negotiable; lead with the cost of the current situation; simplify |

## Objection-answer FAQ line
