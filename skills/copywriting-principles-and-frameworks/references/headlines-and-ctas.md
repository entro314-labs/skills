# Headlines and CTAs

Headline formulas, writing rules, scoring (4 U's, 5-part rubric) and the volume-then-select workflow live in `copywriting-headlines-hooks-and-taglines`. Use that skill for headline work; the points below are the minimum for page copy.

- One core message, specific, benefit-led; 6-12 words; about 60 characters for search titles (real limit is pixel width).
- Avoid puns nobody gets, questions the reader answers "no" to, opening with the company name, "we" instead of "you".
- Write many variants (aim for ~25), shortlist 2-3 with a one-line rationale each, test headlines first.
- Match the headline to the ad, email or link that led to the page.

## Subheadline

1-2 sentences that add specificity to the headline: who it is for, in what timeframe, what risk is removed.

## CTA formula

Action verb + what they get + qualifier.

| Weak | Stronger |
|---|---|
| Submit | Get my free quote |
| Sign up | Start saving today |
| Click here | See my results |
| Send | Show me my savings |
| Learn more | See pricing for my team / See [product] in action |
| Get started | Create your first [thing] / Start my free trial |
| Buy now | Get the complete checklist |

- First person ("my") often beats second person ("your"); treat as a hypothesis to test.
- Friction removal in the button or the next line: "Start free trial - no credit card", "Download guide - takes 2 minutes". Only if true.
- Match awareness: "See pricing" for ready buyers; "Learn more" only for the unaware. Use continuity on later steps ("Continue to checkout").
- Placement: hero; after each major value section; right after social proof; after objection handling; sticky on long pages. Always the same action; minimum 3 on pages longer than two screens. One CTA per section.
- Always provide 2-3 alternatives with a short rationale.
- In-product button labels follow `copywriting-ux-microcopy-and-ux-writing`.

## Cold outreach CTA ladder (best first)

1. Specific time: "Does Tuesday at 2pm work?"
2. Low-friction question: "Worth a quick call?"
3. Permission: "Can I send a 2-minute overview?"

In a first touch avoid calendar links (assume high intent before rapport), multiple CTAs and long-demo asks.

## Power words by purpose (use sparingly, with substance)

| Purpose | Words |
|---|---|
| Urgency (only if real) | now, today, limited, last chance |
| Trust | proven, guaranteed, verified, tested |
| Curiosity | secret, revealed, discover, unlock |
| Exclusivity | members-only, invite-only, select |
| Value | free, bonus, included |
| Simplicity | easy, quick, effortless, done-for-you |
