# Testing copy

Test design, sample-size math, integrity checks and ethics are in `copywriting-conversion-optimization-and-page-audits` (`references/ab-testing-statistics.md`). Messaging-angle tests for social and cold email are in its `references/messaging-tests.md`. Rules to keep in mind while writing:

- Test one variable at a time; do not read results early.
- Record what you tested, what won and by how much.
- Test priority (highest typical impact first): headline; CTA text and placement; social-proof type and placement; hero subheadline; price framing and plan names; page length; image or video; colour and design. Email: subject lines first, then body structure.
- Published "typical lift" ranges and conversion benchmarks by traffic source are unsourced; do not quote them.
- Hypothesis form: "Changing X to Y will raise [metric] because [reason from research]."

## Validation without traffic

- 5-second test: checks clarity (what is this, who is it for), not persuasion.
- Comprehension tests: ask readers to restate the offer in their own words.
- Message-resonance panels: show variants to target-audience respondents and ask which they would act on and why.
- Compare draft language against recorded customer language (`research-voc.md`).
