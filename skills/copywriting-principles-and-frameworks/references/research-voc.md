# Research and voice of customer

Never write cold. Collect customer language first, then copy it and refine it. Do not invent language. Verbatim-quote rules, quote categories and the three-source "gold phrase" test: `copywriting-positioning-and-value-propositions` (`references/voice-of-customer.md`).

## Sources, ranked by value

| Priority | Source | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| High | Support tickets and chat logs | Real problems, exact words, recurring confusion |
| High | Sales-call recordings/transcripts | Objections, decision triggers, buying criteria |
| High | Review sites (competitors' negative reviews included) | Frustrations, desired outcomes, exact phrases |
| High | Niche forums, community threads | Unfiltered pain and desire language |
| Medium | Survey answers ("why did you buy / not buy?") | Reasons, triggers, alternatives considered |
| Medium | Competitor landing pages | Claims made and gaps left open |
| Medium | Social comments on competitor posts | Reactions, objections |
| Medium | Your own testimonials | Value language, outcomes |

Working minimums (not statistical thresholds): 3-5 customer interviews, ~50 survey responses, 10-20 support tickets reviewed, competitor reviews analysed.

## Tagging

Tag each quote as one of: pain, desire, objection, trigger, value, emotional. Record the source and how often the phrase recurs. Capture buying criteria and trigger events (what makes them search now) separately from pain language.

## Voice-of-customer brief

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| who | A specific person, not a demographic |
| current_state | Where they are now |
| desired_state | Where they want to be |
| biggest_frustration | In their exact words |
| trigger_event | What makes them look now |
| primary_desire | The one outcome they want most |
| objections | Top 3 |
| proof_they_need | What would convince them |
| words_they_use | Phrases to mirror |
| words_to_avoid | Jargon, internal terms |

## Split-screen method

VoC quotes on one side, draft on the other. Copy the customer's phrasing, tighten it, keep their nouns and verbs.

Raw to copy: "every month I'd spend 8 hours just reconciling data" becomes "Tired of losing 8+ hours every month to spreadsheet reconciliation?"

## Page-brief questions

Page type; the ONE primary action and any secondary action; ideal customer and the problem; objections and what they already tried; differentiation; key transformation; proof points; traffic source; what the visitor saw before the page.
