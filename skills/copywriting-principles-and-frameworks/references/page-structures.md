# Long-form blueprints and section writing

Page orders by page type (landing, homepage, waitlist, demo, direct purchase, comparison, product page), hero formulas, proof placement and forms are in `copywriting-landing-and-website-page-copy`. Element-level rules (CTA, forms, trust, mobile) are in `copywriting-conversion-optimization-and-page-audits`. Each section advances one argument and has one job; each ends with a reason to read on or a CTA.

## Core section jobs

| Section | Job |
|---|---|
| Social proof | Logos, stats, testimonials; near every CTA |
| Problem/pain | Show you understand it (3 pain points) |
| Solution/benefits | 3-5 benefits, not 10 |
| How it works | 3-4 steps |
| Objection handling | FAQ (top 3), comparisons, guarantees |
| Final CTA | Recap, repeat CTA, risk reversal |

## Blueprints

**Enterprise/B2B:** outcome hero; logo bar; business-pain problem; solution overview; use cases by role; security/compliance; integrations; case study with metrics; ROI section; demo CTA. Speak to the buyer's boss, who must justify the spend.

**Product launch:** announcement hero; demo video; 3-5 feature highlights; before/after; early testimonials; launch offer with a real deadline; CTA.

**Weak pattern:** hero, feature 1-4, CTA. A list, not an argument.

**Long-form sales page (13 blocks):** headline; subheadline (who, timeframe, de-risk); opening story; agitation; "there is a better way" insight; what is included (each bullet a mini-benefit); bonuses; 3-5 testimonials with results; price reveal after anchoring; guarantee; FAQ with embedded selling; final CTA; P.S. restating best benefit, guarantee and deadline.

**Product description:** one-line benefit headline; 2-3 sentences (who, problem, differentiator); features each with why it matters to the buyer; one proof snippet; CTA. Benefits before specs; sensory language for physical goods. Full method: `copywriting-product-descriptions-and-ecommerce-copy`.

## Pricing page copy

Name plans by customer ("Solo Creator" rather than "Starter") when it clarifies; list benefits, not specs; badge the recommended plan; show annual savings in currency, not percent; guarantee below pricing; social proof beside the buy button.

## VSL timing (about 5 minutes)

Hook 0:00-0:10 ([surprising result] + [why keep watching]); problem 0:10-0:45; agitate 0:45-1:30; solution 1:30-3:00 (teach, do not pitch); proof 3:00-4:00; offer and guarantee 4:00-4:30; CTA 4:30-5:00. Longer variants scale each block; keep the order.

## Section writing patterns

- Problem openers: "You know the feeling...", "If you're like most [role]...". Then frustration, time or money lost, impact.
- Benefit block: outcome headline, 1-2 sentence mechanism, proof point.
- How it works: 3-4 numbered steps, each a simple verb plus an outcome ("Connect your tools (2 minutes)").
- Vary section types so the page is not one repeated pattern: proof, story, comparison, FAQ, demo, use cases.
- Analogy form when it clarifies: "Think of X as a digital whiteboard where...".
- Sell the destination, not the process: "Fill out our comparison form" becomes "See how much you're overpaying".

## Lead-gen flow (PAS + RTCA)

PAS for the problem, then RTCA: Reduce friction, Trust fast, Clarify what happens next, Act with a benefit-driven CTA. Objection microcopy near the button: cost -> "Free", "No credit card required"; spam -> "Unsubscribe anytime"; commitment -> "No contracts", "Cancel anytime"; effort -> "Takes 2 minutes". Spell out the next 2-3 steps after the CTA.
