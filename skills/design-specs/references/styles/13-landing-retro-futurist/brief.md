# Retro-Futurist: Brief

The short version of this style. The exhaustive specification starts at [index.md](index.md).

Bridging 1960s space-age optimism with modern capabilities.

### Visual System

**Colors:**
```css
--bg: #0D1B2A; /* Deep space navy */
--text: #E0E1DD;
--orange: #F77F00;
--teal: #00B4D8;
--cream: #EAE2B7;
```

**Typography:**
- Geometric sans-serifs (Futura, Avant Garde)
- Stencil fonts for headings
- ALL CAPS with wide letter-spacing
- Retro numerals

**Elements:**
- Atomic/orbit motifs
- Starburst shapes
- Oval and pill shapes
- Diagonal lines and stripes

### Animation
- Orbiting elements
- Pulsing glows
- "Scanning" line effects
- Typewriter text reveal
