# Press Release Types

Pick the type, then include every mandatory element. Each type needs a news angle that is stated in the lead.

| Type | News angle | Mandatory elements | Timing / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product launch | What is new; which problem it solves that could not be solved before | Product name plus one-sentence value; exact price, date, regions, channels; quantified problem; 3-5 differentiator bullets maximum; quote from CEO/CPO plus a customer if available | Embargoed briefings 2-3 weeks before launch; wire release on launch day |
| Funding | Use of the money | First paragraph: amount, round type, lead investor, use of funds. Also: investor thesis quote, total raised, ARR/users/growth, concrete hiring or expansion targets | Early in the week (Tuesday preferred). Concrete plans ("open a Paris office in Q1 2026"), not "expand internationally" |
| Partnership | What the combination enables that neither party can do alone | Both names in headline; scope (exclusive, geographic, time-limited); quotes from both; both boilerplates; mutual approval of content | Double dateline: `PARIS and SAN FRANCISCO -- (Date) --` |
| Event | Why attend | Name, date(s), location or platform, registration link and price, key speakers, audience, agenda highlights | 4-6 weeks ahead for major events, 2 weeks for small; reminder 1 week before with a new angle (speaker, sold-out tier) |
| Crisis | Accountability and corrective action | Care-Control-Commitment (below) | Holding statement immediately; detailed follow-up as facts emerge |
| Executive hire | Mandate of the new role | Name, title, start date, reports-to, 2-3 relevant prior roles, strategic mandate; CEO quote on why; new executive's quote on why they joined | Quote-pair structure |
| Award | Credibility of the granter | Award name and granting body; criteria or category; selectivity; granter quote if available | Works only with a credible third party; unknown-body awards are ignored |
| M&A | Strategic rationale | Acquirer and target; value or "undisclosed" with deal-structure context; impact on customers, employees, products; closing timeline and regulatory conditions | Public companies: see regulatory checklist |
| Earnings | Results versus prior period | Revenue, net income/loss, EPS (current vs year-over-year); GAAP first, then non-GAAP with reconciliation; guidance; key business metrics | Release before market open or after close, never during trading hours |
| Open-source milestone | Community impact and technical significance | Project name, version, what changed; adoption metrics (stars, downloads, contributors, dependents); benchmarks with methodology; top contributors named in body; changelog, release notes and migration link; license | Zero marketing language. "40% faster JSON parsing", not "significant performance improvements" |

## Crisis: Care, Control, Commitment

1. **Care.** The first sentence after the dateline expresses concern for the named affected groups (customers, residents, passengers). No operational detail first.
2. **Control.** What is known, what has been done, what is unknown. Be explicit about gaps.
3. **Commitment.** Specific next steps with a timeline, update schedule, resources (hotline, web page), third-party investigation if any.

Rules: never "no comment" (say "we are investigating and will share updates"); no speculation or blame before an investigation concludes; no Friday dumps; legal review is required but must not delay the holding statement; use a dedicated media contact, not the general press line.

## Regulatory checklist (listed companies and deals only)

Apply only when the company is listed or the deal is public; this is not general guidance, and legal counsel owns it.

- US M&A: legend under SEC Rule 425, forward-looking statements safe harbor (PSLRA), and an "Additional Information and Where to Find It" section.
- US earnings: furnished on Form 8-K Item 2.02 (within four business days); Regulation G reconciliation of non-GAAP measures; PSLRA safe harbor.
- EU: Market Abuse Regulation applies; national regulators and disclosure channels differ (see regional-conventions.md).
- Ticker in first reference for public companies: "[Company Name] (NYSE: TICK)".

## Partnership and hire patterns

- Partnership lead: "[A] and [B] today announced [what they will do together] for [who], starting [when]." Both parties approve the text before sending.
- Executive hire lead: "[Company] today named [Name] [title], effective [date], to [mandate]." The mandate (what they will do) is the news, not the biography.
