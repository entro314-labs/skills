# Hero, headline, subheadline and CTA pattern bank

## Hero checklist

- Answers: what is it, why should I care, what do I do next.
- Formula: headline (outcome) + subheadline (how) + primary CTA + secondary text link + outcome visual.
- Visual: the real product in use or the real outcome. Never generic stock (handshakes, abstract shapes, faceless laptop scenes). A product screenshot alone is a weak hero if it does not show the result; pair it with the outcome.
- One proof line in or directly under the hero (rating, user count, logo bar, press).
- On mobile the CTA is visible without scrolling and thumb-reachable.
- Anti-patterns: logo sliders inside the hero, autoplay video, "Welcome to our website", price hidden when price is a deciding factor.

## Headline formulas

More formulas and the scoring method: `copywriting-headlines-hooks-and-taglines`.

| Pattern | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome without pain | [Outcome] without [pain] | Get more leads without cold calling |
| Transformation | From [current] to [desired] in [time] | From spreadsheet chaos to a closed month in 3 days |
| Specific number | [Number] [things] to [outcome] | 5 templates to cut your onboarding time |
| Verb-led benefit | [Verb] [outcome] | Ship faster. Close more deals. |
| Question | Tired of [pain]? | Tired of spreadsheets that break? |
| Stop / Start | Stop [pain]. Start [benefit]. | Stop chasing invoices. Start getting paid. |
| Category | The [category] that [differentiator] | The CRM that fills itself |
| Who + outcome + qualifier | [Audience] + [result] + [qualifier] | Cut invoice processing by 70%, without new software |
| Emotion + result + time | [Emotion] + [result] + [time] | Finally relax about payroll, done in 10 minutes |
| Social-proof number | [Number] [people] use this to [outcome] | 10,000 founders use this to get their first 100 customers (number must be real) |

Prefer concrete over abstract; avoid "Unlock your potential", "Transform your business".

Length: headline 6-12 words, subheadline 15-25 words. Primary headline under 12 words.

Headline quality checklist: specific (not "grow your business"); benefit-led; "you" not "we"; one idea; the subheadline adds who, how or proof; passes the screenshot test and the logo-swap test; echoes the traffic source.

For discovery, write 5 variants with different angles (outcome, pain, category, social proof, curiosity) and pick one per audience segment.

## Subheadline formulas

- Method: "[Product type] that helps [audience] [achieve headline promise]."
- Mechanism: "Using [method], [product] [delivers benefit]."
- Contrast: "[Get benefit]. Without [common sacrifice]."
- Use the subheadline to answer the biggest objection when the headline cannot.

## Rewrite pairs

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Welcome to our platform | Send invoices that get paid 2x faster |
| The best project management tool | Finish projects on time without the chaos |
| Next-generation AI solution | Write your marketing copy in 5 minutes |
| We help businesses grow | 10,000 founders use this to get their first 100 customers (real number only) |
| Our software solution enables organizations to optimize their workflow processes | Finish work in half the time |
| Users benefit from our intuitive interface | Start using it in minutes, no training needed |
| We have helped numerous clients achieve their business objectives | 500+ teams ship faster with us (real number only) |

Strong claims need support; every number above is a placeholder pattern, not a value to reuse.

## CTA copy

- Formula: action verb + value (+ risk reducer).
- Outcome of the click, not the form action: "Create account" over "Submit".
- Good: Start free trial; Get started free; See it in action; Create your first report; Try free for 14 days; Get the template; See pricing; Book a 20-minute demo.
- Weak: Submit, Click here, Learn more, Sign up, Register, Download, Contact us.
- First person ("Start my free trial") often wins; test before standardising.
- Friction reducer under the button: No credit card required; Free for 14 days; Setup takes 2 minutes; Cancel anytime; Join 5,000+ marketers (real count).
- Secondary CTA is a text link: "or watch a demo", "See how it works".
- Same primary CTA text everywhere on the page.

By audience: B2B enterprise "Schedule a demo" / "Talk to an expert"; B2C "Get started" / "Try free"; SaaS "Start free trial" / "See how it works"; services "Get a quote" / "Book a call".

CTA placement by position: hero direct action; after features reinforce ("See it in action"); after social proof convert ("Join 5,000+ founders"); final section recap plus urgency or reassurance. Repeat after each major section or about every 1.5-2 screens.

CTA design: high contrast and a color not used for other elements (the 60-30-10 rule: 60% dominant background, 30% secondary, 10% accent reserved for CTAs); at least 44px tall; whitespace around it; contrast at least 4.5:1; full width on mobile, optional sticky bar.

## Benefit and feature copy

- Lead with the outcome: "Save 10 hours a week" over "Automated workflow engine". Put the feature in parentheses or as support.
- Feature block 20-40 words. Each benefit: icon, short title, 1-2 sentences.
- Replace "powerful", "robust", "easy" with the proof: speed, count, time saved, cost.

## Problem section copy

50-150 words. Name the pain in the visitor's words; show a recognisable moment, then the consequence. 2-3 sentences of acknowledgment are enough when the audience is already problem-aware. Do not agitate beyond what is true.
