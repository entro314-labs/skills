# About pages and press-coverage pages or sections

## About page

Purpose: build trust by answering "who are these people and why should I believe them", framed around what the company solves for customers, not around the company's self-image. Put About in the main navigation, not only in the footer.

Essential elements:

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| Company overview | Who, what, where, when, why, how, in a short opening |
| Origin story | The problem that started it; a specific moment; why the founders |
| Team | Photos, names, roles, one line of relevant background each |
| Mission and values | Concrete behaviours, not adjectives; tie to customer outcomes |
| Visuals | Real photos or video of the team, office or work |
| Social proof | Customer testimonials, awards, certifications |
| Contact and links | Contact details, social links, a CTA ("Book a call", "See our work") |

Writing rules:

- Open with the customer problem or the company's reason for existing, then the story.
- Specific facts (year founded, customers served, location) over slogans.
- Real people and photos; no stock team images.
- End with a next step; an About page is still a page with one goal.
- Do not cite statistics about "values-driven buyers" or job candidates unless you have a verifiable source.

Metadata and structure: single H1 naming the company and what it does; title 50-60 characters; description 150-160 characters; AboutPage structured data on the page; Organization structured data on the homepage or root layout.

Deliverable set: outline, origin story, team approach, SEO title and description and H1, trust checklist (footer links present, contact details, certifications, no unprovable claims).

## Brand-page set for designer and artisan stores

For independent designer, handmade or small-batch brands where origin and craft drive trust, build a small set of narrative pages rather than one long About page.

| Page | Purpose | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| About | Positioning in one line, short designer intro | Must |
| Our Story | Origin (place, moment, need), 2-4 values with one sentence each, optional milestones | Must (may be combined with About) |
| Design process / how it's made | Materials, who makes it, steps, quality and care | Should |
| FAQ | Shipping, care, sizing; linked from footer and product pages | Should |
| Sustainability / ethics | Materials, labor, packaging; specific and honest, no greenwashing | Optional, only with a real angle |
| Lookbook | Visual narrative per season or collection, minimal copy | Optional |

- Section pattern per page: opening line, 2-3 short paragraphs (2-4 sentences each), one or two real maker or studio images, one CTA to the next page or to the collection. About plus Story total roughly 300-600 words.
- Navigation: at least one link to the brand narrative in the header; keep main nav to 4-6 items and group Story, Process and Sustainability under one "Brand" item if all exist; footer carries About, Contact, FAQ, Sustainability. The homepage hero or first scroll links to the story or the collection.
- Cross-link so the narrative flows: About -> Story -> Process -> collection. Add an optional "From the designer" line on product pages linking to Story or Process.
- Labels: plain and brand-aligned ("Our Story", "How It's Made"). Use real studio and process photography, not stock.
- Non-fashion craft categories reuse the same structure with the domain swapped. For founder story copy and placement lengths see `copywriting/brand-voice-and-storytelling`; this section covers page structure only.

## Press-coverage: page or section?

| Mentions | Format | Location |
|---|---|---|
| 1-10 | Section ("As seen in") | Homepage below the hero, About page, or footer |
| 10+ | Full page | /press, /news or /in-the-news |

Section rules: logos only, consistent size, high contrast or grayscale, optional single pull quote, optional "See all coverage" link. Avoid too many logos and avoid low-authority outlets; one or two strong outlets beat ten unknown blogs.

Full page rules:

- Order chronologically or group by publication; each item shows headline, outlet, date, link.
- Separate third-party coverage from company-authored press releases.
- Include a media contact and a link to a media kit (logos, product images, founder bios, key facts).
- Dates may be omitted on evergreen coverage.
- Coverage types: news, podcasts, video features, awards, "best X" lists, product reviews.
- Update regularly; stale coverage pages signal a stalled company.

Trust principles: third-party coverage beats self-authored releases; authority of outlet matters; recency matters. Mentions rank below customer results as proof, so place them under customer proof on conversion pages.

SEO: index the page for "[company] press" or "[company] news"; set noindex when coverage is thin.
