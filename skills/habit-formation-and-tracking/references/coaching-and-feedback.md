# Coaching and Feedback

## Tone rules

- Reference the user's own data ("you hit 6 of 7 this week").
- Suggest, do not demand ("you might try...", not "you must").
- Default length 2-3 sentences; expand only when asked.
- Never shame or guilt for missed days. Normalize struggle; judge the pattern, not a day.
- No unsolicited purchases (apps, trackers, equipment) as a first step.
- No false precision about science; avoid "21 days" and "2x" claims (see evidence notes).

## Coaching by streak level

| Streak | Focus |
|---|---|
| Under 3 days | Validate the start; suggest a minimum quota (tiny version); identify friction; offer stacking onto an anchor |
| 3-6 days | Acknowledge survived initial resistance; confirm the cue works |
| 7-14 days | Celebrate; reinforce the pattern; prepare for breakdown points (weekend, travel) |
| 15+ days | Major celebration; identity reinforcement; check sustainability (is the ceiling too high?) |

## Milestones

| Days | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Survived initial resistance | |
| 7 | Short-term habit | |
| 14 | Two weeks of consistency | Do not claim automaticity |
| 21 | Common folk threshold | Oversimplified; do not claim automaticity |
| 30 | Strong pattern | |
| 66 | Median time to automaticity in one study | Individual range is wide |
| 90 | Identity framing ("this is who I am") | Motivational framing only |
| 100 | Triple-digit streak | Round-number marker |
| 365 | One full year | Round-number marker |

The milestone list is a design choice, not research. Celebrate at least 3, 7, 14, 21, 30, 66, 90. Pair with an identity line ("that is 30 votes for being a reader").

## Message templates

After a completion:
> Logged. That is 6 of the last 7 days, and the streak is at 12. The pattern is holding.

After a first miss:
> One miss does not change the trajectory. Tomorrow, just the tiny version: [tiny habit]. The rule is never miss twice.

After two misses:
> Two days is where patterns start to shift, so let us make tomorrow very small: [tiny habit] right after [anchor]. What got in the way?

Milestone near:
> Three more days gets you to 30.

Weekday pattern:
> Saturdays have been the hard day (1 of 5). Want a smaller Saturday version or a different anchor?

Plateau / low motivation:
> Results usually lag effort, and many people stop just before it shows. Your completion rate is 80% over 30 days; that is the work that counts.

## Questions that help diagnose

- When did it last feel easy? What changed?
- Is the tiny version still under 2 minutes?
- Is the anchor still happening every day in the same place?
- What time or situation do the misses cluster on?
- What is the habit giving you (the reward), and is it still delivered?
