## Wind-down tiers (start ~35 minutes before target bedtime)

Pick 2-3 items from each tier. Set a reminder labeled "wind-down start".

| Time before bed | Options |
|---|---|
| ~35 min | Lamps only or dimmed lights. Stop eating. Lay out tomorrow's clothes, bag, keys. Write tomorrow's top 3 priorities. |
| ~20 min | Physical book or e-ink reader. 5 minutes of gentle stretching. Paper brain dump of open worries. |
| ~10 min | Phone face-down on a charger outside the bedroom. Bedroom cool. Clocks turned away. Optional: 4-7-8 breathing (inhale 4 s, hold 7 s, exhale 8 s, 3 rounds) or any slow-exhale breathing. |

Warm bath or shower: the benefit comes from the body-temperature drop afterward, not the warmth. Passive-heating research points to a window of roughly 1-2 hours before bed, so schedule it earlier than the 35-minute ritual, not inside it.

## Caffeine

- Half-life 5-7 h typical; use 6 h as the default. Longer with age over 50, some medications (including some hormonal contraceptives), pregnancy, slow metabolism.
- Cutoff = bedtime minus 12 h. Examples: bed 10:30pm -> 10:30am; bed midnight -> noon.
- Rough content (approximate, varies by brew): green tea 25-45 mg/cup; dark chocolate 12-25 mg/oz; "decaf" coffee 2-25 mg/cup; energy drinks and pre-workouts (read label); some analgesic combinations ~65 mg/tablet.
- Taper rather than stopping abruptly if intake is high (withdrawal headaches).

## Environment checklist

- Dark: blackout curtains or eye mask; cover standby LEDs; no screen glow.
- Cool: 18-20 C (65-68 F). If running hot: fan, lighter duvet, breathable (cotton) sheets, window open.
- Quiet or steady masking noise: fan or white noise; earplugs if a partner snores.
- Bed reserved for sleep (and intimacy). No work, TV, or scrolling in bed.
- Phone charging out of reach.

## Naps

- Max 20 min, with alarm. Finish by ~3pm (or at least 8 h before bed). Chair or couch, not bed.
- Skip naps if there are sleep-onset problems, so sleep pressure builds undisturbed.
- Caffeine-nap: coffee immediately before a 20-minute nap; caffeine takes ~20-30 min to take effect, so waking coincides with its onset. Optional; ignore if caffeine cutoff is already an issue.

## Alcohol

- Faster onset, then REM suppression and fragmentation later in the night.
- Address first when used as a sleep aid. Expect 2-3 worse nights after stopping.
- Do not replace with another sedative strategy; use the wind-down and brain dump.

## Evidence notes

- Wake-time anchor: standard CBT-I practice; mechanism is consistent sleep pressure (adenosine builds with time awake) and a stable circadian phase.
- Social jet lag: weekend shifts of about 1-2 h in sleep timing are associated with worse mood and daytime function.
- CBT-I is the recommended first-line treatment for chronic insomnia by major clinical guidelines.
- A light-emitting e-reader read for hours before bed has been shown to delay melatonin onset and reduce next-morning alertness; the exact delay varies by study.
- Sleep restriction is a core CBT-I component but is not included in this self-guided protocol; leave it to a trained provider.
