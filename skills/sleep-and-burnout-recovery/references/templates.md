# Templates

Worksheets and session templates. Coaches suggest; the person decides.

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Brain-dump template (night)

Set a 10-minute timer. Write unfiltered: worries, tasks, unfinished conversations. Next to each, write either the next physical action and when, or "not tonight". Close the notebook and leave it outside the bed.

## Absolute-minimum list (week 1)

| Must happen this week (max 3) | Deadline | Pushed / delegated / dropped (everything else) |
|---|---|---|

## Energy audit (one work week)

| Activity | Duration | E / N / D | Who was involved | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Review: top 3 D (delegate, reduce, restructure), top 3 E (add back, protect time), best-energy hours (guard for deep work).

## Intake summary template (session 1)

- Sleep: bedtime / wake / onset / wakings / morning feel / alcohol / screens / last caffeine / naps / bedroom.
- Red flags noted: [ ]
- Burnout subscores (date): exhaustion __ cynicism __ efficacy __
- Severity: mild / moderate / severe; referral needed: Y/N
- This week's 1-3 actions: [ ]
- Next check: day 14 (sleep), week 6 (burnout)

## Check-in prompts

- Sleep: "How many of the last 7 days did you hold your wake time? What got in the way on the days you did not?"
- Burnout: "What is one boundary you set this week, and was it held?"
- Both: "What is draining more than it gives, and who could take it over?"
