# Burnout Screen and Drivers

## Concept

Burnout is a response to sustained work conditions, not a character flaw or a lack of resilience. It has three dimensions:

| Dimension | Looks like |
|---|---|
| Exhaustion | Depleted, dreading the day, rest does not restore |
| Cynicism (detachment) | Withdrawn, irritable about work, "what is the point" |
| Reduced efficacy | Doubting competence, feeling nothing you do lands |

## Self-screen (conversation tool, not a validated instrument)

- 12 items, 1-5 frequency scale (never to always), four each on exhaustion, cynicism, inefficacy. Total 12-60. 
- Ask one item at a time, rating the last month from 1 (never) to 5 (every day):

| Dimension | Items |
|---|---|
| Exhaustion | 1. I feel drained at the end of the workday. 2. I wake up already tired at the thought of work. 3. I have little energy left for family or friends after work. 4. Small demands feel overwhelming. |
| Cynicism | 5. I have become more detached or sarcastic about my work. 6. I doubt whether my work matters. 7. I feel irritated by colleagues or clients more than I used to. 8. I do the minimum and no longer care about quality. |
| Reduced efficacy | 9. I feel less competent than I used to. 10. I struggle to concentrate or make decisions. 11. I feel my accomplishments are not meaningful. 12. I doubt my ability to handle the job's demands. |

- All items are worded so that a higher rating is worse, so no reverse scoring is needed. Note the highest subscale (4-20 each).
- Heuristic bands: 12-24 mild (prevention, set boundaries); 25-40 moderate (active recovery); 41-60 severe (consider medical leave, therapy, or major change).

Caveats you must keep:
- The bands and any single-total cutoff are heuristic. They are not cut-offs from any validated burnout inventory; those are licensed instruments scored per subscale against their own norms (not as one total).
- Use the result to decide urgency and conversation topics, never as a diagnosis.
- Severe result or any dark thoughts: professional support first, coaching second.
- Read subscales as well as the total: highest exhaustion, stabilize and cut load first; highest cynicism, look at fairness, values, community and run the energy audit and meaning stage; highest low efficacy, check control, feedback and recognition and rebuild small wins.
- Re-screen every 2-4 weeks and at the week 4-6 decision gate; exhaustion usually moves first, cynicism and efficacy lag.

## Six worklife drivers

| Area | Mismatch | Coaching questions | Typical lever |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workload | Demands exceed capacity or recovery time | What is the weekly must-do list? What could be dropped, delayed, delegated? | Cut scope, renegotiate deadlines, protect off-hours |
| Control | Little say over how or what | Where do you have any latitude? | Reclaim sequencing, meeting attendance, response windows |
| Reward | Effort not recognized, financial or social | What recognition is missing? Is a conversation about pay or scope possible? | Ask explicitly; document contributions |
| Community | Isolation, conflict, no support | Who at work do you trust? | Re-establish one or two supportive contacts |
| Fairness | Unequal workload, unclear process, favoritism | What feels unfair, specifically? | Document; HR or manager conversation |
| Values | Work conflicts with what matters to you | What would you change about what you are asked to do? | Meaning work; role shaping; exit planning |

Rules:
- Focus on what the person can control; name what they cannot.
- Do not rank drivers universally. Lack of control is a strong strain factor in the job demand-control model, but that model is about job strain, not a burnout ranking; do not claim control "beats" workload.
- When several areas are broken and the organization is unwilling to change, that is an environment problem, not a personal one (see exit decision in the recovery plan).

## Distinguishing burnout from depression

- Burnout is tied to work context; depression pervades all areas, including those outside work.
- They can coexist (research shows overlap). If symptoms worsen after 4-6 weeks of real changes, or if there are suicidal thoughts, refer to a doctor and mental health professional.
- Employer-funded counseling may exist; suggest the user check.
