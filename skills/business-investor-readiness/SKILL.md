# Investment Readiness Assessment

Evaluate all materials for investor presentation readiness:

## Assessment Framework

1. **Pitch Deck Analysis**
   - Story clarity and flow
   - Data accuracy and sourcing
   - Competitive positioning strength
   - Financial projection credibility

2. **Supporting Materials Review**
   - Business plan comprehensiveness
   - Financial model sophistication
   - Market research depth
   - Technology documentation quality

3. **Due Diligence Preparation**
   - Data room readiness
   - Reference availability
   - Legal documentation status
   - Operational metrics tracking

4. **Red Flag Identification**
   - Inconsistencies across documents
   - Unsupported claims or projections
   - Missing critical information
   - Potential investor concerns

## Deliverables

- Investment readiness scorecard
- Critical gap analysis
- Recommended improvements
- Presentation optimization suggestions
- Due diligence preparation checklist

Benchmark against successful fundraising materials in the same industry.
