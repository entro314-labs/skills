# Cross-Document Consistency Check

Quickly identify inconsistencies and discrepancies across all documents:

## Analysis Areas

1. **Financial Metrics Consistency**
   - Revenue figures across different documents
   - User/customer counts and growth rates
   - Cost assumptions and burn rates
   - Valuation and funding amounts

2. **Market Claims Alignment**
   - Market size figures across presentations
   - Growth rate assumptions consistency
   - Competitive positioning statements
   - Geographic market priorities

3. **Timeline and Milestone Consistency**
   - Product development timelines
   - Market expansion schedules
   - Funding milestone alignment
   - Partnership implementation timing

4. **Strategic Positioning Alignment**
   - Value proposition consistency
   - Target customer descriptions
   - Competitive differentiation claims
   - Business model explanations

Flag all inconsistencies with specific document references and recommended resolutions.
