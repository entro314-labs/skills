# Seven Sweeps: procedure and examples

Principles: edit, do not rewrite; one dimension per pass; core message unchanged; every edit has a stated reason; keep the author's voice.

## Before the first sweep
- Confirm goal, audience, desired action. Read the whole piece once without editing.
- Questions if context is missing: goal (awareness, conversion, retention)? target action? who is the audience? brand voice (casual, professional, playful, authoritative)? known concerns? what proof exists (testimonials, data, case studies)?

## Sweep 1: Clarity
Look for: unclear sentence structure, ambiguous pronouns, jargon, ambiguity, missing context, sentences doing too much.
Process: read fast and mark, do not correct yet; then propose edits and confirm the intended meaning with the author.
Checks: "Rule of One" (one main idea per section); "You Rule" (copy speaks to the reader, not about the company). These are review labels, not laws.

## Sweep 2: Voice and tone
Look for: formal/casual shifts, inconsistent brand personality, jarring mood changes, "we" vs "the company", humor that undercuts trust. Read aloud. Then return to Clarity.

## Sweep 3: So what
Test: after every statement ask "so what?" and add a "which means..." bridge.
- Fails: "Our platform uses AI-powered analytics."
- Passes: "Our analytics surface insights you'd miss manually, so you can make better decisions in half the time." (the "half the time" must be a true claim, otherwise drop it)
Then return to Voice, Clarity.

## Sweep 4: Prove it
Every claim needs evidence: named testimonials, case studies, statistics with sources, third-party validation, guarantees, customer logos, review scores.
Flag: "trusted by thousands", "industry-leading", "customers love us", results stated without numbers.
Fix: add real proof or soften the claim. Never fabricate.

## Sweep 5: Specificity
Replace vague verbs ("improve", "enhance", "optimize") with numbers, timeframes, examples. Remove content that cannot be made specific.

| Vague | Specific (illustrative; use real figures) |
|---|---|
| Save time | Save 4 hours every week |
| Many customers | 2,847 teams |
| Fast results | Results in 14 days |
| Improve your workflow | Cut your reporting time in half |
| Great support | Response within 2 hours |

## Sweep 6: Heightened emotion
Check for flat informational copy. Levers: pain of the current state, frustration with alternatives, fear of missing out, desire for transformation, pride in a smart choice, relief.
Techniques: paint the "before" state, sensory language, micro-stories, shared experience, reflective questions.
Constraint: emotion must be true to the product and serve the message.

## Sweep 7: Zero risk
Focus on sections near CTAs. List every reason a reader would hesitate, then check each is answered: unanswered objections, missing trust signals, unclear next step, hidden costs, friction.
Risk reducers: money-back guarantee, free trial, "no credit card required", "cancel anytime", social proof beside the CTA, a stated next step ("You'll get an email in 2 minutes"), privacy assurance. Only state guarantees the business actually offers.

## Return path
| After sweep | Re-check |
|---|---|
| 1 | Rule of One, You Rule |
| 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 2, 1 |
| 4 | 3, 2, 1 |
| 5 | 4, 3, 2, 1 |
| 6 | 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 |
| 7 | 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 |

Why loop: adding proof or emotion lengthens sentences and shifts tone, which re-breaks clarity and voice.

## Collaborative loop
1. Run a sweep, present findings. 2. Propose concrete edits. 3. Author decides. 4. Re-check earlier sweeps. 5. Repeat until a full pass finds nothing new.

## Checklist (one line per sweep)
- Clarity: each sentence understood on first read; jargon explained; pronouns clear.
- Voice: formality consistent; personality held; reads well aloud.
- So what: features tied to benefits; no impressive-but-empty lines.
- Prove it: claims substantiated; proof specific and attributed; stats sourced.
- Specificity: vague words replaced; numbers and timeframes present.
- Emotion: pain real; aspiration reachable.
- Zero risk: objections addressed near CTA; next step explicit.
- Final: no typos; consistent formatting; links work; message unchanged.
